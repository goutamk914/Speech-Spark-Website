About Speech Spark

Confidence grows through practice.

A student-led organization helping young students find their voice before the stakes ever feel high.

Who We Are

A supportive first step for young speakers.

Speech Spark is a student-led youth education organization that helps elementary and middle school students build confidence in public speaking, debate, Model UN, and communication. Programs are beginner-friendly, activity-based, and designed to help students practice speaking in a supportive environment.

Students practice in a lower-pressure space before they ever enter a tournament, classroom presentation, MUN committee, or debate round. The goal is confidence first, then skill.

Parent Information

Quick answers before you sign up.

Speech Spark is built for families who want clear details, easy registration, and a supportive learning environment.

  • AgesPrograms are designed mainly for ages 6–13, including elementary and middle school students.
  • What to bringStudents should bring water and a notebook. Any event-specific details appear on the event card.
  • Parent stay policyParents usually do not need to stay. Students often participate more confidently when they practice independently.

Why Speech Spark

Confidence first, then skill.

Public speaking is one of the most common fears, and it rarely gets easier without a safe place to practice. Speech Spark gives students that place: a lower-pressure, activity-based environment where speaking up is normal, mistakes are expected, and encouragement comes from peers who were beginners themselves not long ago.

By the time a student steps into a tournament, classroom presentation, MUN committee, or debate round, they have already done it here first, many times over.

  • Organize ideas clearlyStudents learn to structure a point and make it land.
  • Think on their feetDebate and committee practice build quick, confident responses.
  • Share their voicePresentation practice turns nerves into presence.
