Volunteer Roles

Find your role.

Whether you want to teach, help run an event, or launch a chapter in your own community, there is a place for you on the team.

  • Student instructorLead speaking games, coach practice rounds, and give supportive feedback.
  • Event volunteerHelp set up camps, welcome families, and keep sessions running smoothly.
  • Chapter founderBring Speech Spark to your area with support from the wider team.
  • Sponsor or partnerSupport programs so they can stay free and reach more students.
