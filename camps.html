Speech Camps

Inside our camps.

A full speaking experience — from the first warm-up game to a final showcase where students see how far they have come.

The Experience

Practice first. Confidence follows.

Camps are student-led and beginner-friendly. Students spend the day speaking, debating, and presenting in short, supportive rounds — then finish with a showcase that puts their new skills to work.

01

Warm-up speaking games

Students start with quick prompts and speaking games that make participation feel easier.

02

Debate activities

Students practice arguments, rebuttals, flowing, and thinking on their feet.

03

MUN & diplomacy practice

Students learn how to research, represent a position, and speak in a committee-style setting.

04

Public speaking practice

Students work on clarity, posture, voice, confidence, and audience awareness.

05

Team activities

Small-group activities help students collaborate and become comfortable speaking with peers.

06

Final showcase

Students end with a practice round, presentation, or speaking activity that shows their progress.
