Speech Camps

Inside our camps.

A full speaking experience — from the first warm-up game to a final showcase where students see how far they have come.

The Experience

Practice first. Confidence follows.

Camps are student-led and beginner-friendly. Students spend the day speaking, debating, and presenting in short, supportive rounds — then finish with a showcase that puts their new skills to work.

01

Warm-up speaking games

Students start with quick prompts and speaking games that make participation feel easy. Low stakes, plenty of laughs, and everyone gets a turn before the real work begins.

02

Debate activities

Students practice building arguments, giving rebuttals, flowing a round, and thinking on their feet — the core skills that carry into any tournament or classroom discussion.

03

MUN & diplomacy practice

Students learn to research a topic, represent a country's position, and speak in a committee-style setting, negotiating and finding common ground along the way.

04

Public speaking practice

Students work on clarity, posture, pacing, voice, and audience awareness — turning nervous energy into a steady, confident delivery they can rely on.

05

Team activities

Small-group challenges help students collaborate, listen, and get comfortable speaking with peers, because confidence grows fastest in good company.

06

Final showcase

Students close the day with a practice round, presentation, or speaking activity that ties it all together and shows just how far they have come.
