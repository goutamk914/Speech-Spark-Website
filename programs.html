How Sessions Work

Activity-based, and built for beginners.

Programs are hands-on from the first minute. Students warm up with speaking games, then move into guided practice — a debate round, a mock committee, a short presentation, or a team challenge.

No prior debate, MUN, or public speaking experience is needed. Student instructors give practical, supportive feedback so every student leaves a little more confident than they arrived.

Across Every Program

What every session builds.

Whichever program a student joins, the underlying goals are the same. Skills carry from one program to the next.

Confidence

Students get comfortable being heard, starting small and building up to full presentations and rounds.

Clarity

Organizing ideas, speaking clearly, and using voice and posture so a message actually lands.

Teamwork

Group activities and committee-style practice make speaking with peers feel natural, not nerve-wracking.

Quick thinking

Rebuttals, prompts, and speaking games train students to respond and adapt on the spot.
